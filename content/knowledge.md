# Tahoe Tours and Rentals — knowledge base

Source: https://www.laketahoetoursandrentals.com/

## About
Tahoe Tours And Rentals offers kayaking, biking, hiking, fishing, and more, pairing you
with local guides for real adventures on both the California and Nevada sides of Lake
Tahoe. Based at 152 Highway 50, Stateline, Nevada, 89449. Rated with a TripAdvisor
Certificate of Excellence.

## Water activities
- Kayak / SUP Tours — guided kayak and paddle board tours at Sand Harbor and Cave Rock.
- Sand Harbor Kayak / SUP Tour — the only Sand Harbor tour with transportation from South Lake Tahoe; paddle out to Bonsai Rock.
- Cave Rock Kayak / SUP Tour — history and scenery at Cave Rock. Unavailable for the 2026 season.
- Kayak / SUP Rentals — "Grab 'N Go" rentals with universal roof racks included at no extra cost; delivery available.
- Scuba Diving Tours — guided scuba tours and classes, plus dive certifications, in Tahoe's clear alpine water.
- Fly Fishing / Fly Fishing Tours / Fly Fishing Classes — guided trips and casting lessons with Adventure Guide Brian on private fishing access.

## Land activities
- Hiking — guided hikes to the best viewpoints in Tahoe.
- Mountain Biking / Bike Tours (Hike 'N Bike) — guided and self-guided rides on electric mountain bikes, road bikes, or your own MTB.
- Downhill Thrill (advance/intermediate), The Whole Shebang ("the best mountain bike trail in Lake Tahoe"), Out of the Gate (beginner) — three distinct guided MTB routes.
- Disc Golf — lessons with a coach on a course known as "the most beautiful course in America."

## Tours & events
- Sightseeing Tours / Lake Loop Tour / Virginia City Tour / Emerald Bay Tour — guided drives to the best destinations and views around Lake Tahoe.
- Team Building Events — corporate gatherings and group events.
- Sightseeing/shuttle service is also available for weddings, bachelor/bachelorette parties and corporate events.

## Winter activities
- Moonbikes — electric snow bike / snowmobile touring in the backcountry.
- Snowshoe Tours & Rentals — a laid-back way to explore the Tahoe backcountry.
- Ski Bikes — ski bike rentals, tours and lessons; like mountain biking with skis. Partnered with Skibyk and the American SkiBike Association.
- Ski & Board Mountain Tours — a skiing alternative described as "skateboarding with a ski, no wheels."

## Additional services
- Full ski/snowboard wax and tune service, from hot wax and base grind to core shots, with pick-up and drop-off.

## FAQ
- Will we catch fish on the fly fishing tour? Not guaranteed, fishing depends on conditions, but guides take you to the best private access on the lake.
- Do we need to bring anything? Check the specific tour page's "What To Bring" section; general basics are water, sun protection, and weather-appropriate layers.
- Can we keep the fish? Follow your guide's direction on the day; this isn't specified on the site.
